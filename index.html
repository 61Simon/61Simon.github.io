My Story

I got into web development back in 2021 — not through courses or a bootcamp, but by curiosity. I wanted to make my own website, opened a code editor, and never looked back. What started as a hobby quickly turned into a craft.

Since then, I've built over 50 websites — from landing pages for local businesses to full e-commerce stores. Every project taught me something new, and every client taught me to listen better. I don't just write code — I help people grow their business.

Today I work as an independent web designer and developer. I focus on delivering high-quality work at fair, transparent prices — because great websites shouldn't be a luxury. If you're reading this — I'd love to hear about your project.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?
A landing page usually takes 3–5 days, a business website 7–14 days, and an online store 2–3 weeks. The exact timeline depends on the project scope and how quickly you provide content.
Do you require an upfront payment?
Yes — I take a 50% deposit to start the project, and the remaining 50% is paid after you approve the finished website. This protects both of us.
What do I need to prepare before we start?
Just a general idea of what you want. If you have a logo, brand colors, or text — great. If not, I can help you with copywriting suggestions and design direction.
Do you offer revisions after the site is done?
Every plan includes 2–3 rounds of revisions during the design and development stage. After launch, I also provide free support for 30–60 days depending on your plan.
What about hosting, domain and SEO?
I'll help you set up hosting and a domain — you own them, no lock-in. Basic SEO (meta tags, sitemap, fast loading, mobile optimization) is included with every project.
How do your prices compare to the market?
My rates are below the market average — mostly because I work independently without agency overhead. Same quality, faster delivery, and personal attention, at a fair price.
